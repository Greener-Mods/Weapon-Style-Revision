# Weapon-Style-Revision

### **INSTALLATION**
                 
This is a WEIDU mod thus currently is compatible with other mods.

	1 - Extract the contents of the zip file into your override folder

	2 - Run WeiDU and install

### **WEAPON STYLES REVISIONS**

This mod that adds a third slot to all the weapon styles and is available for single class fighters only (excluding Barbarians)

TWO-WEAPON: This fighting style allows the character to use two melee weapons at the same time with fewer penalties. A character wielding two weapons without a slot in this fighting style would incur a -6 penalty to attack rolls with their primary hand and a -8 penalty with the off-hand weapon. 

Proficient (1 slot): The character's penalties are reduced to -4 with their primary weapon and -6 with the off-hand weapon.
Specialized (2 slots): The character's penalties are reduced to -2 with their primary weapon and -4 with the off-hand weapon.
Master (3 slots): The character's penalties are reduced to 0 with their primary weapon and -2 with the off-hand weapon.

TWO-HANDED: This fighting style allows the character to use a two-handed melee weapon and receive special bonuses.

Proficient (1 slot): The character receives a +1 bonus to damage, a +1 bonus to Speed Factor, and a +1 bonus to critical hit chance. 
Specialized (2 slots): The character receives a +2 bonus to damage, a +2 bonus to Speed Factor, and a +1 bonus to critical hit chance.
Master (3 slots): The character receives a +3 bonus to damage, a +3 bonus to Speed Factor, and a +1 bonus to critical hit chance.

WEAPON AND SHIELD: Anyone can pick up a shield and get its basic protection bonuses, but by spending slots on this fighting style, an adventurer can maximize the benefits received.

Proficient (1 slot): The character receives a +1 bonus to Armor Class and an additional +1 bonus vs. missile attacks.
Specialized (2 slots): The character receives a +2 bonus to Armor Class and an additional +1 bonus vs. missile attacks.
Master (3 slots): The character receives a +3 bonus to Armor Class and an additional +1 bonus vs. missile attacks.

SINGLE-WEAPON: This fighting style is for characters who do not wish to use a shield but want some bonus when using a one-handed melee weapon.

Proficient (1 slot): The character receives a +1 bonus to hit and a +1 bonus to Armor Class
Specialized (2 slots): The character receives a +2 bonus to hit and a +2 bonus to Armor Class.
Master (3 slots): The character receives a +3 bonus to hit and a +3 bonus to Armor Class.
